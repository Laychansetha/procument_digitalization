🔑 1. How to Log In

https://procurement.icsserver.com
  1. Open your web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Safari).
  2. Navigate to: https://procurement.icsserver.com
  3. Enter your assigned Username (or Email) and Password.
  4. Click Sign In to Operations Portal.
Smart Role Detection: The portal automatically detects your official role upon login and opens your team's workspace with only the features and tools you need.

🌾 2. Field Team Guide

ROLE: FIELD
Workspace View: Field Operations portal tab.
Team Purpose: Review, manage, and verify field quality inspections, farmer payment registration profiles, purchase invoices, and transport dispatches.
  1. Quality Specs Inspection: View and record paddy quality tests (Moisture %, Foreign Matter %, Paddy Variety, and Quality Grade). The system calculates the official Base Purchase Price (KHR/kg) automatically.
  2. Farmer Payment Profile: Register or update farmer family codes, contact numbers, and bank account information (Bank name, account number, account holder name, and passbook photos).
  3. Weighing & Purchase Records: Manage purchase invoices with individual sack weights (kg), additional premiums (KHR/kg), seed repayment deductions, and digital signatures.
  4. Transport Dispatch: Link purchased farmer invoices to transport trucks, enter driver details, upload truck photos, and assign destination warehouses (CACC KPT, Amru Nature KPG, Other). If returned by Warehouse (REJECTED), edit manifest details and resubmit.

🏬 3. Warehouse Team Guide

ROLE: WAREHOUSE
Workspace View: Warehouse Receiving portal with real-time status metric cards.
Team Purpose: Receive incoming trucks from the field, weigh gross and tare scale weights, check cargo manifests, and upload scale photos.
  1. Select Incoming Truck: Review trucks currently en route and click Select Truck to begin the receiving intake.
  2. Inspect Cargo Manifest: Click View Details or Download Truck CSV to audit all loaded farmer invoices, sack counts, and field weights before weighing.
  3. Weighbridge Intake: Enter the full truck weight (Gross Weight) and empty truck weight (Tare Weight). The system computes Warehouse Net Weight (kg) and checks weight variance against field weights automatically.
  4. Upload Scale Photos: Upload clear photos of the Gross Scale Display, Tare Scale Display, and physical Transport Invoice.
  5. Rejections & Resubmissions: If an intake is returned by Inventory (🔴 Returned by Inventory), edit weights or photos and click Resubmit. If cargo issues belong to the field team, click Reject to Field.

📦 4. Inventory Team Guide

ROLE: INVENTORY
Workspace View: Inventory Control queue.
Team Purpose: Assign physical storage locations (silos, stacks, bays) for received paddy shipments and confirm stack inventory records before finance payout.
  1. Pending Intakes Queue: View all shipments that have been weighed and received by the Warehouse Team waiting for storage placement.
  2. Assign Storage Location: Select or enter the physical storage location (e.g., Silo 01, Stack A, Warehouse Bay 3) and add quality or moisture observation notes.
  3. Confirm Inventory: Click Confirm Inventory to verify stack placement. This automatically advances the shipment to the Finance Team for disbursement.
  4. Handle Rejections: If scale weights or photos are incorrect, click Reject to Warehouse. If Finance returned a record (🔴 Returned by Finance), update storage notes or return to Warehouse.

💳 5. Finance Team Guide

ROLE: FINANCE
Workspace View: Finance Disbursement table with workflow status filters.
Team Purpose: Audit scale weight reconciliation, verify farmer bank account details and passbook photos, authorize disbursements, and mark payments complete.
  1. Audit & Reconcile Shipment: Open any shipment marked 📦 Inventory Recorded to inspect weight variance (Field Net Weight vs. Warehouse Net Weight) and review linked Farmer Payment Profiles and bank passbook photos.
  2. Authorize Payout: Once scale tolerance and bank documents are verified, click Verify to approve payment and mark disbursement complete (✅ Payment Complete).
  3. Print Reports: Click Print Summary to generate printable payment vouchers for accounting and bookkeeping records.
  4. Reject to Inventory: If bank details or weight data require correction, click Reject to Inventory and enter the audit reason.
