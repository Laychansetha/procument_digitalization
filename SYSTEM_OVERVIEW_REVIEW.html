Module 1: Quality Inspection Specs Record

Physical lab measurement evaluation conducted prior to purchasing to determine paddy grade and calculate the official final purchase price per kilogram.

Key Inspection Parameters:

  • Moisture Content (%): Max 13.5% (A1/White), Max 14.0% (A2/B1/B2), Max 12.0% (Seed).
  • Foreign Matter (%): Dirt, straw, stones (Max 6% standard, Max 1% Seed).
  • Whole Grain % & Broken Rice %: A1 (≥75% whole), A2 (≥70%), B1 (≥60%), B2 (≥50%).
  • Impurity / Red Rice %: Evaluated for Foundation and Certified seed varieties.
  • Organic Status Toggle: Certified Organic eligibility flag.

Pricing Calculation Formula:

Final Price (KHR/kg) = Base Price + Organic Bonus

Example: Phka Rumduol Grade A1
Base Price = 1,650 KHR
Organic Bonus = 0 KHR (or configurable premium)
Final Price = 1,650 KHR/kg
ℹ️ Reusability Rule: When a Specs Record is used for a purchase, it becomes locked from accidental editing, but remains selectable again for future purchases from the same harvest batch.

Module 2: Farmer Payment Profile Registration

Registers official banking credentials, mobile numbers, and passbook photographic proof to guarantee zero errors during electronic bank transfers.

Core Fields & Attributes:

  • Composite Identity: Primary key pair Family Code + Village.
  • Bank Selection: ABA Bank, ACLEDA, Wing, Canadia, Sathapana, etc.
  • Account Number: Validated numeric bank account string.
  • Account Holder Name: Exact name registered with the bank.
  • Relationship to Farmer: Self, Spouse, Son, Daughter, Representative.
  • Passbook Document Photo: Compressed camera scan stored in /public/uploads/.
💡
Uncoupled Lifecycle Invariant: Farmer Payment Profiles and Quality Specs Records are completely independent. A farmer can register their bank book months before harvest, or register at the scale. When the same Family Code + Village is entered, name and location autofill instantly.

Module 3: Paddy Purchase Receipt & Net Payment

The official legal transaction record between IBIS Rice and the farmer. Handles sack weighing, seed deduction calculation, and touch signatures.

1. Total Gross Paddy Value = Σ(Sack Weights) × (Final Price/kg + Distance Premium)
2. Returned Seed Weight = Seed Borrowed × 1.10 (10% standard seasonal interest)
3. Seed Deduction Amount = Returned Seed Weight × Final Purchase Price/kg
4. Net Farmer Payment (KHR) = Total Gross Paddy Value - Seed Deduction Amount

Sack Weight Recording:

Individual bag weights are recorded directly from the field scale (e.g., [102.5, 105.0, 98.0, 101.5]) and serialized into the PurchaseItem.sackWeights JSON column for full auditability.

Standard Purchase ID Format:

Standardized via src/lib/purchase-id.ts: FamilyCode_DDMMYY_24H (e.g. TB010_261026_10). If multiple purchases occur in the same hour, auto-suffixed with _2, _3.

Module 4: Transport Truck Dispatch & Manifest

Consolidates individual farmer purchases into a secure truckload manifest for shipment from village buying stations to the central milling facility.

Transport Attributes:

  • Transport ID Standard: TruckPlate_DDMMYY (e.g. 3A-8899_261026).
  • Destination Warehouse: Field-assigned destination (W002, W003, W004, W005).
  • Truck Cleanliness Protocol: Mandatory checkbox verifying tarpaulin and truck bed are clean of chemical contaminants.
  • Loaded Invoices: Many-to-one association locking purchases into IN_TRANSIT.
⚠️
Locking & Editing Constraint: Once a purchase is assigned to a truck dispatch, it is locked from edits. However, if the Warehouse rejects the truck back to the Field, all linked purchase records unlock automatically for field correction.

Module 5: Warehouse Scale Intake & Tolerance Verification

Executed at the central mill weighbridge scale by warehouse receiving inspectors upon truck arrival. Compares field dispatch cargo weight against certified mill scale readings.

Warehouse Net Weight = Scale Gross Weight (Truck + Paddy) - Scale Tare Weight (Empty Truck)
Weight Difference (Kg) = Warehouse Net Weight - Field Total Weight
Weight Variance (%) = (Weight Difference / Field Total Weight) × 100

Arrived Warehouse Tracking:

Trucks may be diverted mid-journey due to mill silo capacity. The system strictly stores the actual arrival facility in WarehouseIntake.arrivedWarehouse, while preserving the original field dispatch plan in TransportRecord.destinationWarehouse.

Tolerance Thresholds:

  • 🟢 Normal (Green): Variance ≤ 1.0% (or ≤ 300 kg). Within normal drying/transit moisture loss.
  • 🟡 Warning (Yellow): Variance > 1.0% (> 300 kg). Slight variance, flags inspection.
  • 🔴 Critical (Red): Variance > 2.0% (> 500 kg). Severe discrepancy; blocks payment until investigation.

Module 6: Inventory Inspection & Recording

Independent physical verification by the Inventory Team before paddy is legally entered into warehouse storage stock and unlocked for Finance payment.

Verification Protocol:

  • Inspect scale indicator photos (Gross Scale, Tare Scale, Invoice).
  • Confirm grain sacks condition and bag counts.
  • Record physical warehouse storage bin / bay location (e.g. Silo 02, Stack B, Bay 4).
  • Set status to INVENTORY_RECORDED.
🔒
Finance Verification Barrier: The Finance module enforces a strict programmatic block. If a truck has not achieved INVENTORY_RECORDED status, the Finance verification and payment buttons remain disabled.

Module 7: Finance Reconciliation, Verification & Disbursement

Final audit gate where Finance officers reconcile purchase contracts, scale manifests, passbook photographs, export ABA bulk transfer files, and record disbursement proofs.

Standardized CSV Manifest Export Pattern:

Strictly formatted for bank upload and audit across Warehouse, Inventory, and Finance:

{truck_plate}_{ddmmyy}_{warehouse_id}_{username}_{date}.csv

Examples:
Warehouse: 3A-4455_281026_W002_phearun_2026-10-29.csv
Inventory: 3A-4455_301026_W002_bousong_2026-10-30.csv
Finance: 3A-4455_311026_W002_chanvoleak_2026-10-31.csv

Disbursement Workflow:

  • Verify truck data -> status transitions to VERIFIED.
  • Optional currency converter (KHR ↔ USD) with real-time exchange rate input.
  • Export ABA Bulk Transfer / ACLEDA Bulk Transfer CSV.
  • Upload bank payment transfer receipt image/PDF.
  • Click Confirm & Process Payment -> transitions to DISBURSED.

4. Field App Architecture & Offline Synchronization

Flutter Native

4.1 Mobile Field Application Stack

The field tablet application is written in Flutter Native (Dart) and runs on rugged Android tablets deployed with field teams. It operates 100% offline using a local SQLite embedded engine (sqflite).

Local SQLite Engine
Stores all specs records, farmer profiles, purchase invoices, and transport manifests locally with zero network connection.
Dual Signature Canvas
Captures touchscreen digital signatures for both farmer and field inspector; serialized directly as Base64 strings.
ESC/POS Bluetooth Printing
Prints physical thermal receipts in the field immediately after weighing via portable battery-powered Bluetooth thermal printers.

4.2 Bidirectional Synchronization Protocol

Executed when field teams return to the station or connect to Wi-Fi/4G. Implemented in field_app/lib/services/sync_service.dart.

Step 1: Auth & Pull
Master Data Sync
Downloads latest villages, commercial banks, price specs, and tolerances from /api/config.
➔
Step 2: Specs Push
Push Specs Records
POSTs unsynced specs to /api/specs; marks local SQLite row isSynced = true.
➔
Step 3: Profiles Push
Push Farmer Profiles
Uploads passbook photos to /api/upload, then sends farmer profiles to /api/farmers.
➔
Step 4: Purchases Push
Push Purchase Invoices
POSTs purchase receipts with sack weight arrays and digital signatures to /api/purchases.
➔
Step 5: Manifests Push
Push Transport Dispatches
POSTs truck manifests and links purchase IDs to transport dispatch in /api/transport.
🔄
Cache & Data Lifecycle Invariant: Once records are successfully pushed to PostgreSQL, field inspectors can safely use clearSyncedData() to free memory on the Android tablet. The central PostgreSQL server is the authoritative single source of truth.

5. Database Structure & Relational Schema

PostgreSQL + Prisma

5.1 Database Overview & Tables Catalog

Engineered using Prisma ORM v6 on PostgreSQL 15. All relational tables include performance indexes (@@index) on primary query dimensions (family code, village, status, created at).

Model / Table Category Primary Key Foreign Keys & Relations Key Attributes & Business Role
User Master id (cuid) Linked by all transactional tables Stores 30 official users with roles (Role enum) and assignedProvinces.
Village Master id (cuid) Unique name Stores 61 official target villages across Preah Vihear, Stung Treng, Ratanakiri, Mondulkiri.
Bank Master id (cuid) Unique name Commercial banks (ABA, ACLEDA, Canadia, Sathapana, Wing, PRASAC, AMK, Hattha, Campu).
PaddyPriceSpec Master id (cuid) Unique (variety, grade) Base prices (KHR), max moisture %, max foreign matter %, min whole grain %.
LocationRegistry Master id (cuid) Unique name Warehouses (W002, W003, W004, W005) and Buying Stations (ST-PVH, ST-STR, etc.).
FarmerProfile Transaction id (cuid) CreatedBy → User(id) familyCode, village, bank name, account number, passbook photo URL.
SpecsRecord Transaction id (cuid) CreatedBy → User(id) Moisture %, foreign matter %, whole grain %, broken rice %, grade, finalPrice/kg.
PurchaseRecord Transaction id (cuid) TransportRecord(id) [SetNull]
CreatedBy → User(id) 		familyCode, netPayment, seedDeduction, signatures, status (PENDING...DISBURSED).
PurchaseItem Transaction id (cuid) PurchaseRecord(id) [Cascade] Paddy variety, grade, sack count, total quantity (kg), sackWeights (JSON string array).
TransportRecord Transaction id (cuid) CreatedBy → User(id) Driver name, truck plate, field weight, destinationWarehouse, payment receipts.
TransportLot Transaction id (cuid) TransportRecord(id) [Cascade] Manifest lot breakdown by family code, variety, sacks, and field weight.
WarehouseIntake Intake id (cuid) TransportRecord(id) [1:1, Cascade] Gross/tare scale weights, net weight, weight variance kg/%, arrivedWarehouse, scale photos.
RejectionLog Audit id (cuid) User(id) [Cascade] Target entity, targetModule (FIELD/WAREHOUSE/INVENTORY), rejection reason, resolution status.
AuditLog Audit id (cuid) User(id) [Cascade] Immutable action log (CREATE, UPDATE, DISBURSE, INTAKE, CONFIG), IP address, details JSON.

5.2 Key Foreign Key Invariants & Cascades

🛡️
Transport to Purchase Unlinking: PurchaseRecord.transportRecordId uses onDelete: SetNull. If a truck manifest is cancelled or deleted, associated purchases are not destroyed—they gracefully return to unassigned status.
🗑️
Composite Item Cascade: PurchaseItem and TransportLot use onDelete: Cascade. When a purchase or truck is deleted, its individual line item weights clean up automatically without orphan records.

6. Rejection Chains & Operational Return Delegation

Approval Loop

6.1 Linear Single-Stage Rejection Architecture

To prevent chaos and ensure clear chain of custody, the rejection workflow follows a strict linear backward delegation rule. Rejections never jump stages.

Single-Stage Rejection Delegation Loop
Stage 4: Finance
Rejects strictly to Inventory
Status: Returned by Finance
➔
Stage 3: Inventory
Can fix or delegate to Warehouse
Status: Returned to Warehouse
➔
Stage 2: Warehouse
Can fix scale/photos or reject to Field
Status: Returned to Field
➔
Stage 1: Field Team
Updates purchases/manifest & Resubmits
Resets to EN_ROUTE (Forward)

6.2 Rejection Resolution Mechanics

  • Audit Trail Preservation: Every rejection creates an immutable RejectionLog entry containing the rejecting staff name, role, timestamp, target module, and mandatory rejection explanation reason.
  • Automated Resolution: When the responsible team updates and resubmits their corrected record (e.g. Warehouse updates scale weights via PUT /api/warehouse/intake/[id] or Field resubmits via PUT /api/transport/[id]), active rejection logs automatically transition to RESOLVED with the resolving user's identity.
  • Zero State Invalidation: Other completed trucks and historical disbursements remain completely unaffected when one truck is undergoing rejection correction.

7. System Review Checklist & Feedback Guide

QA Ready

Please review the following verification dimensions and use this checklist to record any discrepancies, missing requirements, or operational adjustments needed before further development.

✓ Checklist 1: Business Logic & Calculations
  • Are the moisture, foreign matter, and whole grain percentage thresholds in PaddyPriceSpec 100% matched to current lab standards?
  • Is the seasonal seed return interest rate formula (Seed Borrowed × 1.10) exactly what is required for all cooperatives?
  • Is the default scale tolerance threshold (1.0% / 300 kg warning, 500 kg critical) appropriate for wet season vs dry season transport?
✓ Checklist 2: User Roles, Permissions & Scoping
  • Are all 30 staff members assigned to their correct official roles and provincial scopes in username_roles.csv?
  • Is the single-stage rejection delegation (Finance → Inventory → Warehouse → Field) fully aligned with physical management hierarchies?
✓ Checklist 3: Exports & File Naming Formats
  • Confirm the CSV export naming convention: {truck plate number}_{ddmmyy}_{warehouse id}_{username}_{export csv date}.csv.
  • Confirm the column headers in the Finance export (Order Date, Delivery Date, Warehouse Code, Supplier Code, Supplier Name, Bank Account, Phone Number).
